# ChineseScript

The two ways of writing Chinese that the app offers. Numbers are read the same way in
both; only four characters differ:

| | Ten thousand | Hundred million | Two (before a measure word) | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Simplified` | 万 | 亿 | 两 | zh-CN (Mainland China) |
| `Traditional` | 萬 | 億 | 兩 | zh-TW (Taiwan) |

The currency words are written alike in both scripts: 美元 (US dollar) and 美分 (US cent).
