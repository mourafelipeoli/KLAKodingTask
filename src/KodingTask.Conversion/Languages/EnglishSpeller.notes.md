# EnglishSpeller (en-US)

Reproduces the six examples of the task statement word for word, which fixes the style:

- **No "and" inside the number**: "one hundred one", "nine hundred ninety-nine" (American
  usage; British English would say "one hundred and one"). The only "and" joins dollars and
  cents.
- **Hyphens for 21–99**: "twenty-five", "ninety-nine".
- **Singular only for exactly one**: "one dollar", "one cent"; zero is plural ("zero
  dollars"), as in the task.
- **Zero dollars with cents keeps the dollars**: "zero dollars and one cent", as in the task.
- **No "one" dropped before scale words**: "one hundred", "one thousand", "one million".
