# ItalianSpeller (it-IT)

| Amount | Output |
|---|---|
| 1 | un dollaro |
| 25,1 | venticinque dollari e dieci centesimi |
| 0,01 | zero dollari e un centesimo |
| 45 100 | quarantacinquemilacento dollari |
| 1 000 000 | un milione di dollari |
| 999 999 999,99 | novecentonovantanove milioni novecentonovantanovemilanovecentonovantanove dollari e novantanove centesimi |

## Rules

- **Everything below a million is one word**: "quarantacinquemilacento". Millions are
  separate words: "un milione", "due milioni".
- **"mille" for one thousand, "-mila" from two**: "mille", "duemila", "quarantacinquemila".
- **Elision before "uno" and "otto"**: the tens lose their final vowel, "ventuno",
  "trentotto", "novantotto".
- **"cento" keeps its vowel** ("centouno", "centootto") **except before "ottanta"**
  ("centottanta", "duecentottantotto"). Both are the forms Italian dictionaries list first;
  "centotto" and "centoottanta" also occur.
- **Accent on a final "tre"** in a compound: "ventitré", "centotré", "milletré",
  "ventitré milioni". The single word "tre" has no accent.
- **"di" after an exact million**: "un milione di dollari", "due milioni di dollari".
- **Singular only for one**: "un dollaro", "un centesimo"; zero is plural ("zero dollari").
- **"ventuno dollari"**, not the apocopated "ventun dollari": both are correct, and the full
  form is the written norm for amounts.
- **The cent is "centesimo"**, the usual Italian name for a hundredth of a dollar or euro.
