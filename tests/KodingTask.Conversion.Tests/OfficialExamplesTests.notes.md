# OfficialExamplesTests

The six examples of the task statement, in every supported language.

The English expectations are copied verbatim from the task PDF; they are the acceptance
criteria. The other languages answer the same six questions, so a reviewer can compare the
behaviour across languages line by line. Belgian and Swiss French share expectations here
because none of the examples contains 80; their difference is covered in
`LanguageRulesTests`. Chinese, Japanese and Korean answer the same six questions in their
own scripts; their rules about zeros, "one" and "two" are covered there too.
